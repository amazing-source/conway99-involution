## A. Headline findings
1. **The dossier's "new congruence" was empty.** "The number of induced C4 is odd in the involution branch" (and "diamonds ≡ 0 mod 4") holds in
   *every* srg(99,14,1,2): λ = 1 forbids diamonds, so each of the 4158 non-edges is a diagonal of exactly one induced C4 and c4 = 2079 is a constant.
   More generally, all order-4 induced counts are constants in srg(n,k,1,2) (only #K4 is free in an SRG, and it is 0 here), so the census parameter n3
   is not an order-4 count. Removed from the dossier and replaced by a remark (`rem:c4`). The true part (the σ-invariant induced C4 are exactly the
   21 cycles u v σu σv with D(u) = {v, σv}) is kept.
2. **The model's validation was nearly empty; a strong control now exists.** The k=4 orbit model has 3 primary variables and the k=6 UNSAT is a known
   non-existence, so neither can detect a modelling error. The Berlekamp–van Lint–Seidel graph srg(243,22,1,2) (Cayley graph on F_3^5, ternary Golay
   code) with the involution x ↦ −x (exactly one fixed point) is the m = 11 instance of the same model. Result of `code/z2/control_bvls.py` (light mode):
   rigidity lemmas hold; (R),(D),(I),(P) violations 0,0,0,0; the Q-only and signed equations hold; D is a perfect matching; the general-k analogue of
   Spec(Q_out) ({20, (−2)^10, 4^55, (−5)^44}) and of the F2-rank theorem (55 and 44) are exactly as predicted.
   The CNF-encoding check (`--cnf`, 28.3 M clauses) ran out of memory here and must run on the lab machine.
3. **Prop. F3(iv)** claimed trivial Smith forms for Q − 3I over Z3; the proof only covers S (E14 and E−4 are not 3-integral). Restricted to S.
4. **The rank-20 statement is a consequence of the model**, not "genuinely additional information" (English report, French notes, working note said so;
   the dossier already had it right). Corrected everywhere.
5. **Novelty/credit:** the "flip" lemma is essentially Makhnev's lemma |Fix(t)| = 1 + |Fix(t) ∩ [a]| + 2l (Bielefeld notes, l = 0); a1(t) = 14 is in
   Makhnev–Minakova. The rooted reduction is classical (Cesarz–Woldar 2023, Keramatipour 2026, Thakkar–Severini 2026). What appears new is the
   matching D and the exact orbit model. Keramatipour (arXiv:2604.23037, SAT on C99, "incapability of SAT solvers") was mentioned in the English report
   but missing from every bibliography; added. Crnković–Maksimović's abstract confirms they exclude Z6, S3, Z9 and E9.
6. **Order-7 audit re-verified offline**, from the archived JSONs only: printed formulas leave −94 034 160; with the four corrections the sum is exactly
   C(99,7) with zero n3/z11 coefficients; the multiset of corrected (a, b, 4c) equals the 208 records of `repo_family_affine.json`; the admissible set
   recomputed from the corrected formulas equals `feasible_n3_z11.json` exactly (4159 values, 0 ≤ n3 ≤ 4158, z11 ∈ [ceil4(2 n3), 4 n3] step 4).
   (The printed formulas give the same admissible set; the typos do not change feasibility.)
